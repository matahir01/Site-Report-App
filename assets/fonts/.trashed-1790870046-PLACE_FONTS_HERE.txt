Put the actual font files here before building:
  assets/fonts/Roboto-Regular.ttf
  assets/fonts/Roboto-Bold.ttf

pubspec.yaml already declares these two paths under flutter -> fonts,
and lib/services/pdf_report_service.dart already loads them via
rootBundle at PDF-generation time. Until the real .ttf files are
placed here, `flutter run`/`flutter build` will fail with a missing
asset error, and PDF export will throw at runtime even if the build
somehow succeeds.

Get them free from Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto
(download family -> use "Roboto-Regular.ttf" and "Roboto-Bold.ttf" from
the "static" folder). Delete this text file once the real fonts are in
place.
